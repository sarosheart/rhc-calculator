# Angio Views data — sources, licences and what we did to them

Every file in this folder is generated by `tools/anatomy/` (Python) or `scripts/build-angio-visibility.mjs` from the
raw datasets below. `MANIFEST.json` records each output's hash and the hashes of the inputs it was built from.

## BodyParts3D 4.0 — heart, great vessels, thorax, native coronary reference
"BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International" (CC BY 4.0).
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ — whole-body set `isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99`. (The 4.0 OBJ headers still
carry the older CC BY-SA 2.1 JP notice; DBCLS's licence page states CC BY 4.0.) The heart 5.0 set is not used.
**Modified:** selected parts voxelised at 1 mm, re-meshed, smoothed and decimated; the ventricle wall split into LV and
RV by nearest cavity; the first 30 mm of the ascending aorta split off as `aortic_root`; great vessels clipped to
115 mm either side of the cardiac isocentre; everything moved into DICOM LPS millimetres. The conus artery (FJ2670) and
the left internal thoracic artery (FJ1972) were reduced to centrelines. Files: `heart*.glb`, `thorax*.glb`,
`frame.json`; part of `coronary-*.json` (conus) and `grafts.json` (LIMA chest-wall course). Script: `build_heart.py`,
`bp3d_coronary.py`.

## ImageCAS-X — the coronary trees
Bransby KM, Paulsen RR. ImageCAS-X: a dataset and benchmark for coronary artery segmentation and centerline extraction
in coronary CT angiography. Zenodo 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.21887809, CC BY 4.0.
**Derived:** the labelled centrelines of 800 coronary CT scans were aligned by generalised Procrustes (773 accepted)
and averaged per dominance class (right 706, left 39, co 28; the ramus pooled across classes), then seated on the
BodyParts3D epicardium with one smooth deformation field. No individual patient's tree is shipped.
Files: `coronary-right.json`, `coronary-left.json`, `coronary-co.json`. Script: `build_coronary.py`.

## Dodge 1992 and 1988 — vessel calibres
Dodge JT Jr, Brown BG, Bolson EL, Dodge HT. Lumen diameter of normal human coronary arteries.
Circulation 1992;86:232–246. — Intrathoracic spatial location of specified coronary segments on the normal human heart. Circulation
1988;78:1167–1180.
**Used:** Table 6 (main segments, by dominance) and Table 7 (branches, medium length group) transcribed and checked
twice (2,570 numbers); every radius in `coronary-*.json` is interpolated from those rows. Branch distal thirds and the
septals beyond S3 are extrapolated and marked ESTIMATE.

## CARDIAG — real angiogram frames
Lau DB, Malinowski H, Szyjut J, Brzeski A, Dziubich T, Targoński R, Figatowski T, Zielińska N. CARDIAG: A Dense Segment
Classification Benchmark of Deep Learning Architectures for Coronary Angiography. Zenodo 2026,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.19958730, CC BY 4.0.
**Modified:** 160 of the 644 key frames selected by angle coverage, auto-levelled (21 faint ones further stretched about
the grey levels of their X-ray field) and re-encoded as WebP; their SYNTAX segment masks vectorised to outlines (`cardiag/overlays.json`); the recorded C-arm angles
of the 642 frames that show one coronary artery (two show both) counted into `cardiag/density.json`. Frames were
screened for burned-in text (none found) and reviewed by eye before publication. No DICOM identifiers are included.
Files: `cardiag/`. Script: `build_cardiag.py`.

## Vascular Model Repository — bypass graft geometry (aggregate measurements only)
The data used herein was provided in whole or in part with Federal funds from the National Library of Medicine under
Grant No. R01LM013120, and the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, National Institutes of Health, Department of
Health and Human Services, under Contract No. HHSN268201100035C. Wilson NM, Ortiz AK, Johnson AB. The Vascular Model
Repository: A Public Resource of Medical Imaging Data and Blood Flow Simulation Results. J Med Devices 2013;7(4):040923,
doi:10.1115/1.4025983. www.vascularmodel.com
**Used:** means of measurements we made on the 16 post-CABG models (vein-graft ostium height above the sinotubular
junction and azimuth, take-off and anastomosis angles, lengths, calibres). **No VMR geometry is included** — the grafts
in `grafts.json` are synthesised from those numbers. Script: `build_grafts.py`.

## SYNTAX segment definitions
Sianos G, Morel MA, Kappetein AP, et al. The SYNTAX Score: an angiographic tool grading the complexity of coronary
artery disease. EuroIntervention 2005;1:219–227; segment definitions as published in the SYNTAX score Appendix I
(syntaxscore.org).
