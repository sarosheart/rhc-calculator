SEP & DFP — Pressure Tracing Reference
Aortic Valve (AVA)
mmHg (transaortic gradient)
sec/beat → sec/min
SEP = ejection time/beat × HR. Typical: 0.30–0.35 sec/beat → 20–25 sec/min. Measured from AV open to dicrotic notch. Type over the SEP to use a measured value — it is kept, and clearing it hands the box back to the ejection time.
Mitral Valve (MVA)
mmHg (transmitral gradient)
sec/beat → sec/min
DFP = filling time/beat × HR. Typical: 0.40–0.50 sec/beat → 30–40 sec/min. Shortened in tachycardia. Average ≥5 beats in AFib. Type over the DFP to use a measured value — it is kept, and clearing it hands the box back to the filling time.

Critical AS≤ 0.6
2020 ACC/AHA VHD Table 13: severe AS is Vmax ≥ 4 m/s or mean ΔP ≥ 40 mmHg. An aortic valve area ≤ 1.0 cm² is typical but not required in stages C and D1, and is a defining criterion in low-flow, low-gradient severe AS (D2, D3). An area of exactly 1.00 cm² therefore belongs in the severe band — this ladder is inclusive at 1.0 exactly as the mitral ladder is inclusive at 1.5. Only that boundary comes from the guideline: Table 13 grades mild and moderate AS by velocity and gradient and prints no valve-area bands for them, and it defines no critical-AS stage, so the ≤ 0.6, 1.0–1.5, 1.5–2.0 and > 2.0 rows are catheterization convention.

SeverityMVA (cm²)
Normal4 – 6
No mitral stenosis2.5 – 4
Progressive MS1.5 – 2.5
Severe MS≤ 1.5
2020 ACC/AHA VHD: severe MS is a valve area ≤ 1.5 cm² (Stage C/D) — the threshold every Class 1 PMBC recommendation is keyed to. That boundary is the only row here the 2020 document defines. The others are catheterization convention and are printed as such: the guideline puts no ceiling on Stage B progressive MS (it is any area above 1.5) and has no “no MS” band. A “very severe MS (≤ 1.0)” sub-tier appears in pre-2020 texts only; Table 16 has no such row, so this page no longer prints one (retired 2026-09-21, after Echo Calc and the waveform tool). They exist so that the label printed beside a number is true across the whole reportable range (0.4–6 cm²), not because the guideline draws them.
Gorlin is flow-dependent. Low CO → falsely small valve area. Tachycardia → major effect on MVA (short DFP), minimal on AVA. Use Fick CO for Gorlin.
